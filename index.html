01 / About

Strategy, content & creative execution.

Strategy in one hand. Creativity in the other.

I'm Sarang — a Social Media Marketer and Associate Digital Marketing Executive focused on content strategy, creative campaigns and digital marketing.

I work with brands to plan, create and manage their digital presence — from content calendars and social creatives to reels, Meta Ads, reporting and brand videos.

Since joining Intellyze Innovations in September 2024, I've worked across 10+ brands and industries, handling different parts of their digital and social media presence.

My approach combines strategy with creative execution — understanding what a brand needs to communicate, turning that into engaging content, and using digital campaigns to take it further.

Photography and videography are also part of my creative toolkit, helping me bring a stronger visual perspective to brand content.
